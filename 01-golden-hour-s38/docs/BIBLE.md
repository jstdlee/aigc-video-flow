# GOLDEN HOUR: scene 38 "The Squeeze": continuity bible

Every keyframe and every generated shot is checked against this page. If a frame breaks any rule below, it is regenerated.

## Story
Wildfire smoke grounds the medevac helicopter. Ex-rally driver **Eli Navarro** has 60 minutes to drive a donor heart to the
hospital, where his estranged daughter is already on the operating table. The broker who lost the heart sends his wheelman,
**Kaz**, to take it back. Scene 38 (10 s) is the moment Kaz catches him on the interstate.

## World
- **Road:** six-lane interstate, three lanes each way, **right-hand traffic** (US). Seen from a driver: **concrete median barrier on the LEFT**,
  a yellow edge line along the median, white dashed lane lines, and a **white edge line + steel guardrail + grass bank on the RIGHT**.
  Oncoming traffic only beyond the median, facing the other way.
- **Lanes (our side, left to right):** fast lane (next to the median), middle lane, right lane.
- **Sun:** low golden-hour sun **ahead** of the direction of travel (driving into the sunset); shadows fall back toward the camera behind the cars; warm rim light and lens flare.
- **Landmarks in order:** green sign gantry (passed during shot 4, **behind** the cars from shot 5 on), then a concrete overpass (the cars pass under it in shot 8).
- **Everything moves.** Every vehicle is always driving forward inside a lane; nothing stops, parks or faces the wrong way.

## Keyframe QA checklist (all eleven must pass)
1. Travel direction: every car on our side faces away from the median's oncoming traffic, in the direction of travel.
2. The median is on the drivers' left, and the shoulder and guardrail are on their right.
3. Each car is in the lane given in the blocking chart.
4. Seating: Eli on the left of car A, Kaz on the left of car B, nobody outside a car.
5. Mirrors and windows show what physics says (a door mirror shows the road **behind**).
6. Car A and car B match refs/cars.jpg exactly (body, wheels, wing, colour).
7. Eli and Kaz match refs/people.jpg (face, hair, cap, wardrobe).
8. Props and traffic match (the same truck, the same sedan, the cooler on the passenger seat).
9. Landmark order and sun direction are correct (gantry behind after shot 4, overpass ahead, sun ahead).
10. Start vs end: the intended action happens, and nothing else changes.
11. Background direction matches travel: beside a side-on car the background streaks sideways; a road receding into depth behind a side-on car means the car stands across the road (reject). Added 2026-10-03 after shot 6.
