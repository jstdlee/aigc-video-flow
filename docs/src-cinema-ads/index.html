Blender to Cinema Ads
Field guide · product ads

Blender gives control. The video model gives life.

Expert teams do not ask one tool to do everything. They lock the product, camera and light in 3D. Then they use an AI video model for realism, motion and atmosphere. Then they edit, grade and add sound like a real film.

Blender owns

  • Exact product shape, scale, logo and label
  • Camera position, lens, path and timing
  • Light direction and reflections on glass and metal
  • Repeatable shots: same geometry for every angle

The video model owns

  • Photoreal surfaces, skin, liquids, steam, smoke
  • Natural motion physics and camera feel
  • Environments and people you cannot build fast
  • Fast variants: new hooks, sizes, locations

Seven stages, one direction of flow

Every Higgsfield guide repeats one rule: lock assets first, generate video last. Blender adds a stronger lock, because 3D geometry cannot drift.

Stage 3–5 in depth

How experts build the product shot in Blender

The Higgsfield blog covers its Blender plugin, not classic Blender craft. This part fills that gap with standard product-viz practice. The goal is one thing: a frame so correct that the video model only has to add life.

Model Studio practice

  • Start from CAD (STEP → mesh) or measured photos. Work in real-world scale (metres).
  • Add bevels to every hard edge. Real objects never have razor edges, and bevels catch highlights.
  • Use Subdivision Surface with support loops for smooth plastic and glass.
  • Keep the logo and label as a UV-mapped texture, never as AI-generated text.

Materials Studio practice

  • Principled BSDF with real values: glass IOR 1.45–1.52, metal = Metallic 1.
  • Add micro-detail: a faint roughness map, fingerprints, dust. Perfect surfaces look like CG.
  • Liquids inside glass need their own volume and IOR (water ≈ 1.33).
  • Check colour against the brand swatch under neutral light.

Light Studio practice

  • HDRI for base reflections, then large Area lights as softboxes.
  • Key + rim + long strip lights. Strips draw clean lines on glass and chrome.
  • Use light only from motivated sources. This matches the "Practicals" idea in Cinema Studio.Higgsfield
  • Colour management: AgX view transform for film-like highlight roll-off.

Camera Studio practice

  • Set a real sensor (36 mm) and real focal length: 50, 85 or 100 mm macro for products.
  • Turn on Depth of Field. Use a focus object, and set f-stop to 2.8–5.6.
  • Animate with one move per shot: dolly, orbit or push-in. Ease in and out on the Graph Editor.
  • Use the same numbers in the AI prompt later (lens, height, speed).

Render Studio practice

  • Cycles for final frames. Denoise on. Render at 2× the target size.
  • Export passes: beauty, depth (Z), Cryptomatte / object mask for the product.
  • The mask lets you put the exact product back over the AI video in compositing.
  • Render the first and last frame of each move. These are your start/end keys.

  A 15-second spot
  
Shot structure: hook, appetite, payoff

  
A 15 s ad needs 3–5 shots: one hook, one or two detail shots, and one payoff.Higgsfield Cut on action. Use hard cuts, not transitions.

  

    
0–2 s · HookMacro of a water drop hitting the cap. Sound first.

    
2–5 s · TextureExtreme macro push-in across the label. f/2 shallow focus.

    
5–8 s · MotionPour / splash / steam. The "alive" moment, mode B.

    
8–12 s · HeroSlow 180° orbit, contre-jour rim light. Mode A composite.

    
12–15 s · PayoffLocked-off packshot, logo, call to action. Pure Blender render.

  Your first project
  
A practice plan: one bottle, one week

  
    
    
  1. Write the brief. One product, one feeling, five shots, 15 s, 16:9 and 9:16.
    2. 
    
  2. Model the bottle in Blender. Real scale, bevels, glass IOR 1.5, label as a texture.
    3. 
    
  3. Light it. HDRI + one soft key + two strip lights. AgX view. Render one still.
    4. 
    
  4. Animate the camera. One orbit, one push-in. 85 mm, f/2.8. Render start and end frames, and a product mask.
    5. 
    
  5. Generate. Image-to-video from your start frame. Test at 720p (~$2 / 8 s), then 1080p (~$4 / 8 s) for winners.Higgsfield
    6. 
    
  6. Composite the hero. Put the Blender product back over the best AI plate with the mask.
    7. 
    
  7. Finish. Cut on action, grade to one reference still, add SFX before music. Export each aspect ratio.
